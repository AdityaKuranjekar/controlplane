Design Decision: Recall Over Precision
This system deliberately prioritizes recall. It is tuned as a hallucination safety net rather than a low-alert filter — ensuring hallucinated medical, legal, or enterprise financial claims are intercepted before reaching users, even at the cost of some false positives.
🔬
Conformal Prediction Explained
For each α (target false-negative rate), the calibration finds the NLI score threshold τ such that among the calibration set, at most α% of true hallucinations pass through. The chosen τ is then applied at runtime — any response with NLI entailment score below τ is flagged or blocked.
